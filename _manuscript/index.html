1.1 The prototype

The OCR is a R&D LLM processed experiment by Chloë Farr in cooperation with Computational Publishing Service NFDI4Culture @ TIB Open Science Lab with HsH.

This prototype made by Simon Worthington as part of the Linked Open Exhibition an experimental prototype pipeline for publishing art exhibition data as Linked Open Data (LOD). It was created with students of the Bachelor of Arts Information Management at Hochschule Hannover in the course BIM-126-02 Data Science (SoSe 2026).
